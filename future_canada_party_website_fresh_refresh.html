Our Five Priorities

Our party focuses on essential services, affordability, good use of taxpayer money, and job opportunities.

01
EDUCATION

Stronger public schools

Students deserve learning environments that help them build knowledge, confidence, and useful skills.

To achieve this, we will:

  • Work with provinces and territories to fund classroom supplies and extra learning support.
  • Give students better access to digital tools and hands-on technology lessons.
  • Help students explore careers, skilled trades, and options after high school.
Overall goal: Give more students the tools to succeed.
02
HEALTHCARE

Care people can access

Healthcare should be easier to navigate, with attention to both physical health and mental well-being.

To achieve this, we will:

  • Help provinces and territories hire and keep healthcare workers.
  • Support proven ways to improve mental health, especially for young people.
  • Use federal health funding to support shared goals and check that money is used well.
Overall goal: Help people access care and support healthier communities.
03
HOUSING

More homes, more opportunity

Increasing housing supply requires cooperation, predictable investment, and fewer unnecessary barriers to building.

To achieve this, we will:

  • Use federal funding to help build more homes and needed infrastructure.
  • Work with local and provincial governments to reduce delays in approving new homes.
  • Check that projects build homes and provide services communities need.
Overall goal: Help communities build more homes and give people more options.
04
CLIMATE & ENERGY

Innovation for a cleaner future

Canada can reduce emissions while working to maintain reliable, affordable energy for households and businesses.

To achieve this, we will:

  • Support research into renewable energy, nuclear power, and ways to store energy.
  • Support Canadian researchers and businesses developing ways to cut pollution.
  • Choose projects based on cost, reliability, evidence, and environmental impact.
Overall goal: Support clean technology and reliable energy.
05
JOBS & ECONOMY

Skills, businesses, and good jobs

A strong economy depends on people having useful skills and businesses being able to innovate and grow.

To achieve this, we will:

  • Support apprenticeships, job training, and work opportunities for young people.
  • Make it easier for small businesses to find information and government support.
  • Support Canadian ideas and research, and check whether programs are working.
Overall goal: Help people prepare for work and help businesses grow.
The plan

Our first steps

Our party will be transparent about how it plans to pay for its decisions. We would assess the cost of proposals before committing public funds and report on results openly.

Our financial approach

Spend carefully. Invest where it matters.

Our party would aim to keep federal finances sustainable, protect important services, and make careful investments in Canada's future.

Our political perspective ↓
1

Review spending

Review existing programs to find repeated work, ineffective spending, and ways to deliver services more efficiently.

2

Set clear priorities

Give priority to clearly defined goals supported by credible evidence, with a clear explanation of how Canada benefits.

3

Cost policies before launch

Be open with the public about the costs of our spending, and explain why it is beneficial before making major changes.

4

Measure and report results

Publish public reports to show what is working, what is not, and how we are working to make our plans succeed.

5

Work with other governments

Coordinate with provincial, territorial, and municipal partners so programs are funded and delivered properly to all citizens.

Our values

Our political perspective

The Future Canada Party sits in the political centre. It combines support for public services and economic opportunity with a focus on careful spending, business growth, and other similar activities.

Our Economic Perspective

We support responsible public spending. The public spending our party supports should have clear goals and costs that can be justified for the majority of citizens.

Our social perspective

We value individual freedom, fairness, and equality. We consider the viewpoints of many citizens and the effects that changes may have.
